# Privacy

This page says what fafam.us keeps about you when you sign in, and why. fafam.us is the sign-in service for the Fafam apps, such as the photo app at withme.you.

## What fafam.us keeps

- **Your sign-in method:** a phone number, an email address, or the account id that Google or Apple gives us. We keep it so that you get the same account each time.
- **The email that Google or Apple reports.** We keep it to show you which account you used. We never join two accounts because their emails match.
- **Your username and your name.** You choose them, and the other people in your rooms see them.
- **Your sign-in sessions.** We keep a hash of each session and of each app token, never the token itself, with the times of their use.

We keep no password, because fafam.us has none. We keep no tokens from Google or Apple. We ask Google and Apple only for your account id, your email and your name.

## Who else sees it

- **Twilio** sends the code by text message or by email, and checks it. Twilio sees the phone number or the email address.
- **SendGrid**, part of Twilio, delivers the email.
- **Google or Apple** sees that you signed in to fafam.us, if you chose them.
- **Fly.io** runs fafam.us and its database.

We sell nothing about you, and we show no ads.

## What you can do

- **Hide your email from us** with Sign in with Apple. Apple then gives us an address that forwards to yours.
- **Ask us to delete your account,** or to send you what we keep, at the address below.
